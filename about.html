You belong in STEM.

We believe every child deserves the chance to discover what they're capable of. Our job is to make sure curiosity has a place to grow through hands-on experiences, the right tools, and people who look like them leading the way.

Read our why
💡 Why we exist

Three things we know for sure

01

Every child is born curious. We aim to create opportunities for that curiosity to grow through exploration, encouragement, and STEM experiences.

02

Talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. Early exposure helps children discover what they are capable of and what they can become.

03

Children imagine futures they can see. We aim to help students explore the many possibilities within STEM through hands-on learning and mentorship.

🌱 What makes us different

We build an ecosystem

A child's curiosity grows through the support of a community. We are bringing together students, families, educators, and mentors to create a stronger foundation for STEM learning.

We equip parents

We aim to assist families create more opportunities to ask questions, explore ideas, and discover STEM together.

We give educators tools

We are building resources designed to support educators as they create more opportunities for STEM exploration in their classrooms.

We bring STEM stories closer

Representation is essential. Kids need to see people who look like them in STEM.
