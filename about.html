Our Story ✨

How a question at Harvard turned into a mission to bring STEM to every child.

The Spark 🌍

We're three Nigerian women who met at Harvard during a summer program. For the first time, we saw what happens when kids get into STEM early. The labs, the mentors, the careers we'd never even thought about.

It changed everything.

The Question 💡

But we also realized something: so many kids never get that chance. Not because they're not curious, but because no one showed them the path. We asked ourselves: "What if we had these opportunities earlier? And how can we give that to other kids?"

The Mission 🚀

That question became STEM Starts With Us. We set out to bring hands-on STEM education to kids who don't always get the chance. Through kits, storybooks, workshops, and eventually podcasts and mentorship, we're working to make sure every child can see themselves in STEM.

The "Us" 💜

STEM Starts With Us, and "us" is bigger than the three of us. It's parents who want more for their children. Teachers who go the extra mile. Mentors who share their journey. Communities that come together. STEM starts with all of us.
