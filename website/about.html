Our Story ✨

Learn about our mission to bring STEM education to every child, everywhere!

How It Started 🌱

STEM Starts With Us grew from the belief that curiosity needs representation to thrive. Too many kids were never shown the many paths STEM could take or the people who walk them.

What We Believe 💡

We believe representation fuels curiosity. When kids see people like them in STEM careers, confidence follows and possibility expands.

Where We're Going 🚀

We’re working toward a future where every child can explore STEM careers and see themselves represented in them. As we grow, we’re expanding our programs, creating new resources, and partnering with communities to reach more kids.
