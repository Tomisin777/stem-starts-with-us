🌍 Born from a Question at Harvard

What If Every Kid Had This Chance?

Three Nigerian women met at a Harvard summer program and saw what was possible when kids get into STEM early. We asked: how do we give that to every child? That question became STEM Starts With Us.

💭 Questions?

Frequently Asked

Here are some answers to help you learn more!

🎯 Why is STEM Education Important?

+
STEM helps kids learn how to solve problems and think for themselves! It also opens doors to some really cool careers and gives them skills they'll use every day.

🧪 How Do We Make Learning Fun?

+
We believe learning should be an adventure! Our programs include cool experiments, hands-on activities, games, and exciting challenges.

👋 Who Can Join Our Programs?

+
All kids are welcome! We especially want to reach kids in communities that don't always have STEM programs nearby.

🌍 Why "With Us"?

+
"Us" means all of us! Parents, teachers, mentors, communities, and the kids themselves. It takes a village, and everyone plays a part.

💪 How Can I Help?

+
You can donate, volunteer, partner with us, or just share what we're doing with people you know. Every little bit helps!
