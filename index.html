Born From a Question 💡

We are a student-led initiative founded by three Nigerian women who met at Harvard. We saw what happens when kids get access to STEM early.

But we also realized: so many kids never get that chance. That realization became our mission.

You Asked, We Answered

We're a non-profit organisation built by three Nigerian women who met at Harvard. We create hands-on STEM kits, storybooks, workshops, and community resources to make STEM accessible and exciting for every child — especially those who don't usually get the opportunity.

Our programs are designed for kids of all ages, with a special focus on children who historically have fewer opportunities to access quality STEM education. We also create resources for parents and teachers to use at home and in the classroom.

There are so many ways! You can donate to fund programs, volunteer your time, partner with us as a school or organisation, or simply follow us on social media and share our mission. Every bit of support helps us reach more kids.

We're based in Baltimore, Maryland, but our mission is global. We're building digital resources and programs that can reach kids everywhere, while also working to make an impact in local communities.
