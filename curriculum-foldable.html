My STEAM Future

An exploration guide for young innovators!

By STEM Starts With Us

S

Food Scientist

What they do: Mix chemistry and biology to invent new flavors of chips, candies, and healthy snacks!

🧪 Try it: Mix lemon juice, sugar, and baking soda. Watch the bubbles and taste your custom soda!
T

Game Designer

What they do: Write code and logic to decide how your favorite characters jump, run, and win.

💻 Try it: Write exact instructions (code) for a friend to walk across the room with their eyes closed!
E

Theme Park Engineer

What they do: Use physics to build super fast, super safe rollercoasters that don't fly off the tracks.

🎢 Try it: Tape paper towel tubes together to build the longest marble run possible!
A

Animation Artist

What they do: Combine art and computers to bring drawings to life in your favorite movies.

🎨 Try it: Draw a character on the corner of 10 notebook pages. Change it slightly on each page and flip them fast!
M

Sports Statistician

What they do: Use math and data to tell coaches which player is most likely to make the winning shot!

📐 Try it: Toss 10 balled-up paper "basketballs" into a bin. Count how many you make. That's your shooting percentage!
